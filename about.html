My core focus is mentorship and management for Branding, Content, and Sales. For clients who need hands-on execution, I also offer support services including post scheduling, caption copywriting, campaign setup, and “as needed” chatting assistance.

In-depth examples of my work can be found in the Case Studies page.
I work exclusively with female-identifying and fem-presenting OnlyFans creators. My expertise ranges from fitness and goth to curvy, petite, and the "girl-next-door" niches. But beyond aesthetics/niches, I work best with creators who possess a growth mindset. If you are open to constructive feedback and ready to elevate your business, we will be a great match.
From 2021-2023, I worked as a corporate social media manager. Then in 2024, I started working exclusively on OnlyFans as a consultant + manager and briefly as a creator. This is how I developed an approach that blends corporate discipline with industry-specific insight.
It reflects my management style. When I step into a client's account, I adopt their persona completely, essentially cosplaying as their fans’ “secret GF.” The name is a nod to that intimate, behind-the-scenes magic I create with clients.
I’m based in the United States and operate primarily within Central Standard/Daylight Time (CST/CDT).
